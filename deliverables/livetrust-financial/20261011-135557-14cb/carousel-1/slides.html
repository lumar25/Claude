First, a quick reminder

SBA loans are made by banks and other approved lenders, and the SBA guarantees part of the loan. The lender still reviews and decides on each application, so preparation matters.

#1

Mistake #1: Starting too late

Mistake: Looking for financing only after signing a purchase agreement or lease. Why it hurts: Less room to compare lenders or prepare for underwriting (the lender's review). Instead: Start the conversation early.

#2

Mistake #2: Asking for the wrong amount

Mistake: Sizing the loan to the purchase price only. Why it hurts: You can miss working capital (day-to-day money) or inventory. Unexplained extras can raise questions. Instead: List what every dollar pays for (use of funds).

#3

Mistake #3: Messy or mixed finances

Mistake: Mixed personal and business spending, or books that aren't current. Why it hurts: Mixing can hide real cash flow. Lenders typically review your financial records together. Instead: Separate accounts, current books, consistent numbers.
